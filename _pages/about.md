Hi there! I am **Vinayak**. I am interested in studying the behaviour of slender rod-like objects at the continuum as well as the nanoscale.

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. degree under the supervision of **Prof. Ajeet Kumar** and **Prof. Prateek Gupta** from the [Department of Applied Mechanics](https://am.iitd.ac.in/), **Indian Institute of Technology Delhi**. Earlier, I obtained my MS(R) degree from the same department, and my B.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering from Delhi Technological University (DTU).

Prior to my graduate studies, I worked as a Design Engineer at Bechtel Corporation focusing on stress analysis.

### Research Interests
- **Slender Rod Theories**: Geometrically exact rod formulations, 3D large deformations, and mechanics of rods.
- **Metamaterials & Lattices**: Mechanics and simulation of complex structural lattices and composite architectures.
- **Continuum & Nanoscale Mechanics**: Multi-scale modeling bridging continuum mechanics and nanoscale behaviors.
